Adding one grain to a sandpile can leave it stable or trigger an avalanche across many sites. This project uses the two-dimensional Bak--Tang--Wiesenfeld model to investigate how that variation arises from a fixed local rule. The report explains scale invariance and the role of boundary dissipation, then proves that a finite open lattice stabilizes uniquely regardless of the legal toppling order. The planned experiments will examine mean height, avalanche distributions, and correlations between topples, area, loss, and spatial extent. Simulation and analysis remain to be completed.
